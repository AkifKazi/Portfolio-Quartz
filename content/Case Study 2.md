#Banking #UX_Research #Team 

Overview

The primary goal was to leverage mobile-specific technology—such as biometrics, cloud connectivity, and local file access—to simplify and streamline the home loan application and funds transfer processes. Our aim was to reduce unnecessary steps and enhance the overall user experience, particularly targeting mobile-first users.  
  
We integrated features like biometrics (fingerprint or Face ID), autofill for personal information, and mobile document scanning to reduce friction in both the application and transaction processes.

User Research

UPI’s success reflects a strong preference for streamlined, fast transactions. Users expect similar ease when managing finances, including loan applications.  
  
There is a general perception that home loan applications are complex and time-consuming, deterring potential users.  
  
Users of the traditional system might be skeptical of a simplified process, questioning its validity or security, especially with sensitive tasks like home loan applications.

We chose to focus on home loans because HDFC is a well-known leader in this area, and improving this process had the potential for significant impact. We identified the funds transfer feature as needing improvement to compete with the intuitive simplicity of UPI.  

Through competitor analysis and studying user reviews, we found that people struggled with lengthy forms and found the beneficiary-adding process in funds transfer unnecessarily complex.

Problem Analysis

Home Loan Application  
Users had to complete lengthy, cumbersome forms on a desktop browser, which were not available on mobile platforms. Many fields were unnecessary, adding to the complexity.  
  
Funds Transfer  
The process of adding beneficiaries was tedious and time-consuming, especially when compared to the fast-paced, intuitive experiences offered by modern UPI platforms.  
 We aimed to appeal to a younger audience accustomed to UPI apps like Google Pay. By simplifying the mobile banking experience, we wanted to make HDFC’s app the preferred platform for online banking.

Ideation

Auto-selection of payment methods (NEFT, IMPS, RTGS) based on the transaction amount and user preference to reduce decision fatigue.  
  
Auto-prefill of primary applicant details for seamless form completion using data from bank servers.  
  
Using the user's date of birth to fetch relevant insurance EMI details.

We approached the problem with a fresh perspective, challenging every step in the existing process. Despite our lack of deep banking knowledge, this outsider’s view allowed us to question long-standing practices and think creatively. We analyzed processes from other banks to benchmark and refine our solutions, eventually crafting more feasible ideas from initially out-of-the-box concepts.

User Testing

Funds Transfer Process  
The beneficiary-adding process was the primary pain point. Users found it confusing and lengthy, especially when compared to the quick UPI experience.  
  
Home Loan Application  
While experienced users appreciated the simplified process, first-time users were skeptical and often mistook the inquiry form for the actual loan form. Both groups felt that the process was "too simple," leading to concerns about legitimacy and missing steps.  

The main challenge to address moving forward is the beneficiary-adding process for funds transfer. Additionally, the home loan application needs to better communicate its purpose to instill confidence and clarity.

Before & After

We drastically reduced the form length and simplified the overall process, resulting in a smoother, more efficient experience. Users completed applications with minimal errors and appreciated the streamlined design.  

While this is a student project and we didn’t measure real-world business impact, the approach demonstrated the potential to make HDFC’s mobile app more competitive in an increasingly UPI-dominated space.

Learnings

Confidence Building: Adding more guidance and context throughout the home loan application process to reduce user anxiety.  
  
Beneficiary Process: Streamlining the beneficiary-adding process for funds transfers, potentially finding a breakthrough method to speed up the process.  
  
Visual Design: Further refining the UI to be more visually appealing and intuitive, making it more competitive against user-friendly apps like Google Pay.

Understanding the banking domain, especially without prior experience, was our biggest hurdle. Learning banking rules and regulations while critically analyzing each step required a significant learning curve.
